# Illustrations by Henrique Alvim Corrêa (1876–1910)

Plates from the 1906 Belgian edition of H. G. Wells' *La Guerre des Mondes*
(translated by Henry-D. Davray; L. Vandamme & Co., Brussels).

- Scans: Duke University Libraries, via the Internet Archive
  (archive.org/details/laguerredesmonde00well) and The Public Domain Review
  (publicdomainreview.org/collection/henrique-alvim-correa-war-of-the-worlds/).
- Rights: Public domain worldwide; no additional rights claimed.

Files are named after the scan's page number (`plate_0111.jpg` = page 111),
resized for the game. One plate (page 177) is intentionally not included.
