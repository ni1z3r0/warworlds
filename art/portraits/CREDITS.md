# Character portraits

AI-generated pen-and-ink illustrations (Figma AI image generation,
gpt-image model), made for this game from H. G. Wells' descriptions of the
characters in *The War of the Worlds* (1898). Where Wells gives little
physical detail (the narrator, Ogilvy, Henderson, the landlord), the
period details were filled in to suit 1898 Surrey.

Source file: Figma, "War of the Worlds - Character Portraits".
